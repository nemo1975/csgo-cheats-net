People search "CSGO wallhack" and "CSGO ESP" like they are different products. In the menu we ship, they are the same nine visual modules: player and bot ESP, box, skeleton, snapline, health, name, distance, max range.

Here is what that looks like in a real round.

## Boxes vs skeletons

Boxes are cleaner. Good for streaming tests and for maps where a lot of players stack in one site. Skeletons help you read movement through a smoke when a box is just a rectangle in the cloud.

On Inferno banana, a skeleton tells you if they are walking up or holding the corner. On Ancient, too many skeletons is just noise. Cap max distance.

## Health and names

Health bars are useful in a retake. Names are optional. If two bars overlap, turn names off. You already know who is on your team from the color filter.

## Snaplines

Snaplines point from your screen toward a player. Handy on Nuke vertical fights. Ugly if you leave them on in Deathmatch. Most people keep them for Competitive only.

## Smokes

ESP does not "clear" a smoke. It draws on top of it. If your overlay is neon and full opacity, a demo reviewer does not need a PhD. Dim it. You still get the information.

## What this plan is not

It does not move your crosshair. That is [aimbot](/products/csgo-cheats/aimbot/). It is not a skin changer. It is not a radar replacement for the in-game minimap, even if marketing pages from other games talk that way.
