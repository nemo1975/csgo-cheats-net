If a CSGO cheat page says "undetected" as a permanent fact, close it. CSGO uses VAC and VAC Live. Player reports still exist. None of that pauses because you paid for a monthly key.

This article is not a bypass guide. It is the risk in plain language.

## What "status" actually is

The banner on [csgocheats.net](/) is an operator saying "this build matches the current game and anti-cheat we tested today." It is not a legal shield. It is not a forecast.

When status is offline or updating, do not launch. Burning a session "to see if it still works" is how people turn a patch day into a ticket.

## VAC vs VAC Live

VAC is Valve’s long-running anti-cheat. VAC Live is the newer, more aggressive layer people talk about with CSGO. You do not need the internal details. You need to know both can result in account action, and both can change after a game update.

We do not publish detection methods, delays, or "safe hours." That content goes stale and it is usually wrong.

## Demos and reports

Obvious snap aim in a Premier demo is a gift to whoever reviews it. ESP with neon boxes through every smoke is not invisible either. Conservative settings are not "safe." They are less cartoonish.

## What we will not claim

- That Complete is safer than Aimbot or the other way around
- That stream-safe capture hides you from VAC
- That an HWID reset is ban recovery (it is for hardware changes)
- That the [HWID Spoofer](/products/csgo-cheats/) addon undoes a VAC ban. It does not.
