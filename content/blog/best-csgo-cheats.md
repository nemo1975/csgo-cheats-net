Open any CSGO cheat shop and you will see the same three words: undetected, instant, cheap. Fine. None of those words tell you what is inside the download, and that is the part you are paying for.

Here is how to judge a package in about five minutes.

## Start with what the cheat actually does

CSGO is a round-based 5v5 game. Long sightlines, smokes, and one-life rounds. A box ESP and nothing else will not carry you on Mirage mid. A package worth buying covers three areas.

**Aim assistance.** You want FOV, smoothness, max distance and bone selection (head, neck, body, pelvis). Smoothness is the sleeper setting. A hard snap to the head looks ugly in a demo, while a gentler pull is easier to live with. A visible check, so you never lock onto someone behind a wall, is worth having too.

**Visuals.** Player ESP with distance, health bar, name and skeleton is the baseline. Snaplines help on Nuke when someone is above or below you. A max-distance slider keeps the screen readable instead of buried in markers.

**Quality of life.** A config system, menu and unload binds, an FPS limit and a custom crosshair sound minor until you need to change something mid-round. Then they are the whole difference. Our [guide to aimbot settings](/blog/csgo-aimbot-settings/) walks through each slider.

## Status beats promises

No honest seller can promise a cheat stays safe forever. A CSGO patch or VAC Live update can change things overnight. What you can demand is a live status on the product page and a seller willing to say, in plain words, that you should re-check after updates. A page that promises permanent safety is telling you something about itself.

## What happens after you pay

A good checkout is boring. You pick a plan, pay, and the loader and setup guide unlock in your portal. Updates during your key are free. Support answers when something breaks. You also want Windows 10 or 11, Intel or AMD hardware, and an HWID reset in case you change PCs. If you are unsure about the reset, read our [HWID reset walkthrough](/blog/csgo-hwid-reset/).
