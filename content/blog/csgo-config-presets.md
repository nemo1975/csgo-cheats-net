One CSGO cheat config for every mode is how you end up with Deathmatch FOV in a Premier pistol round. The menu can save more than one profile. Use that.

## What to split

**Deathmatch.** Wider ESP range is fine. You are there to test smoothness and spray. Nobody is reviewing the demo like a Major.

**Competitive / Premier.** Tighter FOV, visible check on, ESP max distance down so Mirage mid is not a Christmas tree. Chest bone for rifles.

**Wingman.** Smaller map, fewer players. You can drop snaplines and keep boxes.

## How to save without drama

Name the profiles after the mode, not after a feeling. "premier-rifle" is easier to find at 1 a.m. than "legit2_final_FINAL."

Change one thing, play a map, save. If you tweak eight sliders and then save, you will not know which one made the AK feel weird.

Cloud save is on the Complete misc list. Export/import is the fallback when you move PCs. The forum thread on [configs between PCs](/forum/save-config-profiles-between-pcs/) covers HWID plus cloud.

## After a patch

Old smoothness values can feel wrong on a new build even if the menu opens. Reload the portal preset, then re-apply your named profiles. Do not assume last month’s file is still the one you want.

If you only bought Aimbot or ESP, you have fewer tabs to save. That is not a problem. Just do not mix DM and Premier on the same file.
