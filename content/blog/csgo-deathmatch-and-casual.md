Official bot lobbies are not how you test CSGO cheats. If you want a low-stakes place to try settings, you use Deathmatch. Casual is the slower cousin. Premier is not a lab.

## Deathmatch

Respawn, lots of fights, no economy. This is where you learn if smoothness is too snappy and if ESP range is drowning the screen. Official DM is still other players. It is not risk-free. It is just the mode where a bad FOV costs you a death, not a round.

Keep a DM-only config. Wider ESP is fine here. Copying that file into Premier is how people get clown overlays on Mirage mid. See [config presets](/blog/csgo-config-presets/).

## Casual

Longer rounds, more players, less discipline. Casual is useful if you want to see ESP through executes without the Premier timer in your head. It is a poor place to test AK spray assist because the fights are messier.

## Wingman

Two vs two. Good for checking whether snaplines help on a small map. Bad for deciding your Competitive FOV.

## What not to do

Do not buy a weekly key and spend it all in Premier "to see if it feels real." That is backwards. Status online, DM first, then Competitive if you still want it.

The menu includes "target bots" because some modes and community servers have them. Official matchmaking is still humans. Do not assume a bot filter makes you invisible.

When the loader is new or a patch just landed, DM is also where you find out the menu bind is dead before you waste a ranked queue. The [troubleshooting](/blog/csgo-cheat-troubleshooting/) page is next if it is.
