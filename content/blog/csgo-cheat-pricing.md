CSGO cheat pricing is not mysterious. You are paying for a key length and a module set. Weekly, monthly, and lifetime show up on almost every checkout page. The numbers change; the logic does not.

## What you are actually buying

A **weekly** key is a trial with a clock. Useful if your PC is picky or you have never used an external loader. Bad if you buy it on a patch Friday and status sits offline until Sunday.

A **monthly** key is the default for people who play a few nights a week. You get updates during the window. If the loader needs a rebuild after a CSGO patch, that rebuild is supposed to land while your key is still valid.

A **lifetime** key is one payment for as long as the product is maintained. It is not a promise that VAC Live will ignore you. It is a promise that you will not pay again for that SKU while the shop still ships it.

Prices live on the merchant checkout page, not on this article. We do not reprint dollar amounts here because they move.

## Module set vs duration

Duration is half the decision. The other half is Complete vs Aimbot vs ESP.

If you want both tracking and visuals, paying monthly for Aimbot-only and then wishing you had boxes is a waste. Read the [product hub](/products/csgo-cheats/) first. Then pick a length.

Cloud DMA is billed separately from Complete, Aimbot, and ESP keys. Do not assume a lifetime cheat key includes DMA hardware access.
