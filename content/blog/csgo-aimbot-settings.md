CSGO aimbot menus look simple until you sit in Deathmatch and every slider fights the last one. Smoothness, FOV, bone, and visible check are the four that actually change how a round feels.

This is not a magic config. It is a way to stop guessing.

## Smoothness first

High smoothness makes the crosshair drift toward a target. Low smoothness snaps. Demos punish snaps. If you stream, they punish even harder.

Start smoother than you think you need. Play a few DM rounds on Dust II or Mirage. If you are losing close-range sprays because the assist is late, drop smoothness a little. Do not jump straight to "always on, zero smooth."

## FOV is a circle, not a skill

FOV is the area the aimbot is allowed to care about. A huge circle will grab people you were not aiming at. A tiny circle only helps when you already had the shot.

Rifles can live with a medium FOV. AWP usually wants a smaller one. Save separate profiles if the menu lets you. The [Aimbot plan](/products/csgo-cheats/aimbot/) includes FOV border drawing so you can see the circle while you test.

## Bones

Head, neck, body, pelvis. Head is the obvious pick and the one that looks the worst when it overshoots. Chest with visible check is the setting most people keep after a week.

Closest-bone sounds smart until two players cross. Then it jitters. Leave it off unless you know why you want it.

## Visible check

Turn it on. CSGO is full of smokes and thin walls. If the aimbot pulls through a wall you cannot shoot, you are advertising yourself in the replay.

## A sane first session

1. Load a portal preset, do not invent twelve values.
2. Deathmatch only. Not Premier.
3. Change one slider at a time.
4. Save the config before you queue Competitive.
